## Syllabus

| unit_number | syllabus_content | lectures | tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sample space, probability axioms, real random variables (discrete and continuous), cumulative distribution function, probability mass/density functions, mathematical expectation, moments, moment generating function, characteristic function |  | sample,space,probability,axioms |
| 2 | Discrete distributions: uniform, binomial, Poisson, geometric, negative binomial. Continuous distributions: uniform, normal, exponential. Related problems. |  | discrete,distributions,uniform,binomial |
| 3 | Joint cumulative distribution function and its properties, joint probability density functions, marginal and conditional distributions, expectation of function of two random variables, conditional expectations, independent random variables |  | joint,cumulative,distribution,probability |
| 4 | Bivariate normal distribution, correlation coefficient, joint moment generating function (jmgf) and calculation of covariance (from jmgf), linear regression for two variables. Least squares methods and related problems. |  | bivariate,normal,distribution,correlation |
| 5 | Chebyshev's inequality, statement and interpretation of (weak) law of large numbers and strong law of large numbers, Central Limit theorem for independent and identically distributed random variables with finite variance. |  | chebyshev,inequality,statement,interpretation |
