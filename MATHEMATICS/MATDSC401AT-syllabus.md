## Syllabus

| unit_number | syllabus_content | lectures | tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Group isomorphism, examples, Cayley's theorem, properties of isomorphism, Automorphism, Inner automorphism. Group homomorphism, examples, kernel of a homomorphism, properties of homomorphisms, properties of subgroups under homomorphisms. First, second and third isomorphism theorems for groups. |  | group,isomorphism,examples,cayley |
| 2 | External direct product, properties of external direct product. Group of units modulo n as an external direct product. Internal direct products of a finite number of subgroups of a group, relation between internal direct product and external direct product. Ideals of a ring, examples, ideal test, principal ideal generated by an element, ideal generated by a subset. |  | external,direct,product,group |
| 3 | Factor rings, prime ideal, maximal ideal, related problems. Ring homomorphism, ring isomorphism, properties of ring homomorphism, kernels. First, second and third isomorphism theorems for rings. |  | factor,rings,prime,ideal |
| 4 | Ring of polynomials over a commutative ring, division algorithm, remainder theorem, factor theorem, related problems. Principal ideal domain. Associates, irreducibles and primes in an integral domain, equivalence of primes and irreducibles in a PID, ascending chain condition in a PID. |  | ring,polynomials,commutative,division |
| 5 | Unique factorization domain, Euclidean domain, relations between ED, PID, UFD. Irreducible polynomials, reducible polynomials. Eisenstein's Criterion, Relation between irreducible polynomials, maximal ideals and fields. |  | unique,factorization,domain,euclidean |
