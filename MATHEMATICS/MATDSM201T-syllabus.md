## Syllabus

|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | Inequalities, properties, inequalities involving arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means, Cauchy-Schwarz's inequality, Minkowski inequality, Holder's inequality. |  | inequalities,involving,arithmetic,geometric |
| 4 | De Moivre's theorem, related problems, Expansion of sin nθ and cos nθ, expansion of Sin θ and Cosθ in ascending powers of θ, functions of complex arguments. |  | moivre,expansion,ascending,powers |
| 5 | Gregory's series; summation of trigonometric series; hyperbolic functions. |  | gregory,series,summation,trigonometric |
