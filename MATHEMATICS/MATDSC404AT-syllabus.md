## Syllabus

| unit_number | syllabus_content | lectures | tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Definition of optimization and its importance; Basic terminologies: Design variables, Constraints, Objective function, and Variable bounds; Different types of Optimization Algorithms – based on number of variables, nature of variables, constraints, approaches used and number of objectives, etc. Formulation of different types of unconstrained and constrained optimization problems. |  | definition,optimization,importance,terminologies |
| 2 | Local optimal point; Global optimal point; Inflection point; Optimality criteria; Related examples. Basic idea of Traditional and Non-traditional Optimization Algorithms. Bracketing Methods: Exhaustive search method and Bounding phase method; Algorithms and examples. Region elimination methods: Interval halving method, Fibonacci search method and Golden section search method; Algorithms and examples. |  | local,optimal,point,global |
| 3 | Gradient based methods: Bisection method, Newton Raphson method and secant method; Algorithms and examples. Optimality criteria for Multi-variable functions; Unidirectional search; Direct search method: Simplex search method; Algorithms and examples. |  | gradient,bisection,newton,raphson |
| 4 | Method of multipliers, Kuhn-Tucker conditions for nonlinear programming. Solution of constrained nonlinear optimization problems through Kuhn-Tucker conditions; Problems. |  | multipliers,kuhn-tucker,conditions,nonlinear |
| 5 | Other optimization algorithms: Genetic Algorithms, Particle Swarm Optimization, Ant Colony Optimization and Artificial Bee Colony Optimization; Basic terminologies, working principles and simple problems. |  | other,optimization,algorithms,genetic |
